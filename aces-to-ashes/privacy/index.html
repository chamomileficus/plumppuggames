Privacy Policy — Aces To Ashes

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Aces To Ashes is developed by Plump Pug Games.

The short version

Aces To Ashes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no network features.

What the game stores

Your progress — runs, unlocks, achievements and settings — is saved on your device only, using the operating system's standard app storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we receive

Nothing from the game itself.

While Aces To Ashes is distributed as a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may provide us with crash reports and basic installation and session counts. Testers can also choose to send feedback and screenshots through TestFlight. When you send feedback, we receive what you wrote, the email address associated with your TestFlight account, and information about your device and iOS version. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by the game.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking software of any kind. No data is sold or shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no data, no data is collected from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
